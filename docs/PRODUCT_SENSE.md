# Product Sense

> **Purpose:** Who the users are, what they value, and how to make product trade-offs.
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-11-19

- **User:** a TV owner who knows no code. They can install an app and answer plain questions. They can't read a stack trace.
- **Value:** a calm TV with no junk or ads from the TV's own software, and a home screen that is theirs (photos, clock, name, four apps).
- **Streaming apps are untouched.** Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, YouTube and the rest must keep working. We never promise to remove ads inside them.
- **Ask before every choice.** Never guess which apps they use or which photos they want.
- **Trade-offs:** safety beats speed and features; undo beats completeness; a plain-language message beats a precise technical one.
- **Scope:** Android TV and Google TV only. Samsung, LG, Roku and Fire TV are out.
